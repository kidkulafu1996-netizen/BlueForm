# BlueForm

Gym tracker and exercise guide.

An offline workout tracker that installs on your phone like an app.

- Log sets, reps and weight, with a rest timer and weight suggestions
- Programs you can edit, including a 5-day upper-body-focused split
- Exercise guide with target muscles, form steps and animated demos
- Progress charts, personal records and a training calendar
- Works with no internet once installed; data stays on your phone

## Install on your phone

1. Open the GitHub Pages link for this repo on your phone.
2. **iPhone:** in Safari, tap Share → **Add to Home Screen**.
   **Android:** in Chrome, tap ⋮ → **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).
3. Open it from the home screen icon. It now works offline.

## Your data

Workouts are stored on the phone only. Use ⚙︎ → **Export backup** regularly,
and **Import backup** to move data to a new phone.

## Updating

Replace `index.html`, then change `VERSION` in `sw.js` (for example `blueform-v2`)
so installed phones download the new version.
